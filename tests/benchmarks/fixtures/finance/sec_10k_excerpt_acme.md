## PART I — BUSINESS

### Item 1. Business Overview

ACME Financial Corporation is a diversified financial services holding company with **$847.3 billion** in total assets as of December 31, 2025. The company operates through three primary segments:

1. **Consumer Banking** — Provides deposit accounts, residential mortgages, credit cards, auto loans, and personal lending products to approximately **42 million** households across the United States.

2. **Commercial Banking** — Serves middle-market and large corporate clients with lending, treasury management, capital markets, and advisory services. As of year-end 2025, the commercial loan portfolio totaled **$312.6 billion**.

3. **Wealth and Investment Management** — Provides brokerage, investment advisory, retirement plan services, and trust services with **$3.2 trillion** in client assets under administration.

The company operates **4,217** retail branches across all 50 states and employs approximately **218,000** full-time equivalent employees.

### Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis

#### Revenue Performance

Total revenue increased **$4.6 billion** or **4.3%** year-over-year to **$112.4 billion** for FY 2025, driven primarily by:

- Net interest income growth of **$3.2 billion** reflecting higher loan volumes and expanded net interest margin of **3.21%**, up 13 basis points from FY 2024.
- Non-interest income increase of **$1.4 billion** driven by strong wealth management fees, investment banking advisory fees, and trading revenue.

#### Credit Quality

The allowance for credit losses (ACL) was **$14.7 billion** as of December 31, 2025, representing **1.52%** of total loans. Key credit quality metrics:

| Metric | FY 2025 | FY 2024 |
|--------|---------|---------|
| Net Charge-Off Rate | 0.38% | 0.42% |
| Non-Performing Loan Ratio | 0.67% | 0.73% |
| ACL Coverage Ratio | 1.52% | 1.58% |
| 30+ Day Delinquency Rate | 1.15% | 1.21% |

#### Capital Management

During FY 2025, the company returned **$18.2 billion** to shareholders through:
- Common stock dividends: **$7.8 billion**
- Share repurchases: **$10.4 billion**

The Board of Directors increased the quarterly dividend from **$0.38** to **$0.42** per share effective Q3 2025, a **10.5%** increase.

### Item 9A. Risk Factors

#### Credit Risk
The company's credit exposure is diversified across consumer ($198.7B), commercial real estate ($134.2B), corporate and industrial ($97.4B), and other ($42.3B) portfolios.

#### Market Risk
Value-at-Risk (VaR) for the trading portfolio at 99% confidence, one-day holding period, averaged **$34.7 million** during FY 2025, with a high of **$51.2 million** (November 2025) and a low of **$22.1 million** (March 2025).

#### Operational Risk
Total operational risk losses for FY 2025 were **$187 million**, down from **$224 million** in FY 2024. The largest categories of losses were: execution and process management (34%), external fraud (22%), and clients/products/business practices (18%).

#### Cybersecurity Risk
The company invested **$1.7 billion** in cybersecurity and technology risk management in FY 2025, a **21.4%** increase from $1.4 billion in FY 2024. The Cybersecurity Operations Center processed approximately **45 billion** security events per day.

#### Climate Risk
The company has committed to achieving net-zero greenhouse gas emissions in its financing activities by **2050** and has set interim targets for portfolio emissions reduction of **30%** by 2030. As of year-end 2025, the sustainable finance portfolio totaled **$127.3 billion**.
