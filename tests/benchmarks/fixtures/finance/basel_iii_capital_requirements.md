# Basel III Capital Requirements — Reference Summary

## Document Control
- **Reference**: BIS Basel III Framework, December 2017
- **Version**: Final Standard
- **Effective Date**: Fully phased in by January 1, 2027

---

## 1. Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) Minimum

The minimum Common Equity Tier 1 capital ratio is **4.5%** of risk-weighted assets (RWA) at all times.

Calculation:
```
CET1 Ratio = (CET1 Capital / Risk-Weighted Assets) * 100
```

CET1 capital consists of:
- Common shares issued by the bank
- Stock surplus (share premium)
- Retained earnings
- Accumulated other comprehensive income (AOCI)
- Common shares issued by consolidated subsidiaries and held by third parties (minority interest)
- Regulatory adjustments (deductions from CET1)

## 2. Tier 1 Capital Minimum

The minimum Tier 1 capital ratio is **6.0%** of RWA. Tier 1 capital equals CET1 capital plus Additional Tier 1 (AT1) instruments.

AT1 instruments must meet 14 specific criteria including:
- Instruments must be issued and fully paid up
- Must be subordinated to depositors and general creditors
- Must not be secured or covered by a guarantee
- Must be perpetual with no maturity date
- Must have fully discretionary non-cumulative dividends/coupons

## 3. Total Capital Minimum

The minimum Total Capital ratio is **8.0%** of RWA. Total Capital equals Tier 1 plus Tier 2 capital.

Tier 2 capital includes:
- Instruments issued and fully paid up
- Subordinated to depositors and general creditors
- Original maturity of at least 5 years
- No step-ups or other incentives to redeem

## 4. Capital Conservation Buffer

Banks must hold a capital conservation buffer of **2.5%** of RWA, composed of CET1 capital, on top of the minimum requirements. This brings the effective CET1 requirement to **7.0%**.

The buffer is designed to absorb losses during periods of financial and economic stress. When a bank's capital falls into the buffer range, constraints on distributions (dividends, share buybacks, discretionary bonus payments) are imposed according to a sliding scale.

### Distribution Constraints by Buffer Level
| CET1 Ratio Range | Minimum Capital Conservation Ratio |
|------------------|-----------------------------------|
| > 7.0% | 100% (no constraint) |
| 6.375% – 7.0% | 60% |
| 5.75% – 6.375% | 40% |
| 5.125% – 5.75% | 20% |
| <= 5.125% | 0% (full constraint) |

## 5. Countercyclical Capital Buffer

The countercyclical buffer ranges from **0% to 2.5%** of RWA, determined by national authorities. It must be met with CET1 capital.

As of Q4 2025, the following jurisdictions have activated non-zero CCyB rates:
- United Kingdom: 2.0%
- Norway: 2.5%
- Sweden: 2.0%
- Hong Kong: 1.0%

## 6. Global Systemically Important Banks (G-SIB) Surcharge

G-SIBs must hold an additional surcharge of **1.0% to 3.5%** of RWA in CET1, depending on their systemic importance score. The buckets are:

| Bucket | Score Range | Surcharge |
|--------|-------------|-----------|
| 1 | 130-229 | 1.0% |
| 2 | 230-329 | 1.5% |
| 3 | 330-429 | 2.0% |
| 4 | 430-529 | 2.5% |
| 5 | 530-629 | 3.5% |

## 7. Leverage Ratio

The Basel III leverage ratio requires banks to maintain a minimum **3.0%** Tier 1 capital against total (non-risk-weighted) exposures.

Calculation:
```
Leverage Ratio = (Tier 1 Capital / Total Exposure Measure) * 100
```

The Total Exposure Measure includes:
- On-balance sheet exposures
- Derivative exposures
- Securities financing transaction exposures
- Off-balance sheet items (with uniform 10% credit conversion factor for most)

## 8. Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR)

The LCR requires banks to hold sufficient high-quality liquid assets (HQLA) to cover total net cash outflows over a **30-day** period of acute stress.

The minimum LCR requirement is **100%**, meaning HQLA must be at least equal to total net cash outflows.

Calculation:
```
LCR = (Stock of HQLA / Total Net Cash Outflows over 30 days) * 100
```

HQLA are divided into:
- **Level 1**: Cash, central bank reserves, sovereign debt (0% haircut, unlimited)
- **Level 2A**: Certain government and public sector debt, covered bonds (15% haircut, capped at 40% of HQLA)
- **Level 2B**: Corporate debt, certain equity, residential mortgage-backed securities (25-50% haircut, capped at 15% of HQLA)

## 9. Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR)

The NSFR requires banks to maintain a stable funding profile relative to their assets and off-balance sheet activities over a **one-year** horizon. The minimum requirement is **100%**.

Calculation:
```
NSFR = (Available Stable Funding / Required Stable Funding) * 100
```

Available Stable Funding (ASF) factors:
- Tier 1/Tier 2 capital: 100% ASF factor
- Other capital instruments with residual maturity >= 1 year: 100%
- Stable retail/small business deposits: 90-95%
- Less stable retail deposits: 80-90%
- Wholesale funding from financial institutions: 50%
- Other liabilities: 0%

## 10. Large Exposures Framework

A bank's exposure to any single counterparty or group of connected counterparties must not exceed **25%** of its eligible capital base. For G-SIBs, the limit between G-SIB counterparties is reduced to **15%**.

|-----------|------|
